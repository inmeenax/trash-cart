Our story

The art of thoughtful gifting.

Maison Hampers is an Indian gifting house built around one belief: a beautiful gift should feel like it was chosen, not merely bought.

The Maison point of view

Small details carry the most meaning.

We curate gourmet treats, self-care rituals, keepsakes and festive details into compositions that feel generous, warm and considered.

Our visual world borrows from boutique fashion: quiet neutrals, tactile ribbons, soft blush, subtle gold and just enough drama. Our Indian warmth comes through in the occasions we celebrate and the way we celebrate together.

Our values

Curiosity. Care. Craft.

Curiosity keeps our edits fresh. Care keeps the recipient at the centre. Craft is in the last fold of ribbon, the note card and the way every piece sits inside the box.

We are building Maison slowly, thoughtfully and with room for local makers, seasonal finds and the wonderful messiness of Indian celebration.

Thoughtful gifting, beautifully composed for India's little and big celebrations.
